# Gift output

Every idea carries its own status beside its title. The output opens with a one-line summary of them ("1 of 3 ready to consider"), or `No good fit` when no idea survives. The statuses:

- `Ready to consider`: sourcing, destination, and timing are verified.
- `Concept pending confirmation`: a required purchase, delivery, budget, address, or preference check remains.
- `No good fit`: state whether evidence, appropriateness, or fulfilment failed.

State buyer context and assumptions.

Link each source where it is mentioned: the supplier, the product page, the video timestamp, the web page. Never collect links in a list at the end.

For each of the three materially different ideas, provide:

- Item and rationale.
- Scores from 0 to 2 for personal meaning, usefulness, originality, respect, and feasibility. Give one evidence-based sentence per score. Missing admissible evidence, or a zero for respect or feasibility, vetoes recommendation.
- Verified premise: fact ID, attributed creator, exact excerpt, dated source URL, and checked-on date. A premise from `lasting_favourites` carries the label "lasting favourite, last said <year>".
- Supplemental public evidence, separately labelled.
- Creative inference, explicitly labelled as an inference rather than a creator preference.
- Maker brief: material, dimensions or variant, exact text, artwork and placement, quantity, proof requirement, supplied assets, and rights requiring confirmation. Do not reproduce protected art or logos without permission.
- Purchase route: supplier, exact URL, evidence for destination eligibility, quantity-one availability, customization, stock, price/currency, extras, production, transit, arrival range, buffer, taxes/customs, packaging, and source links for material claims.
- Order card: the preloaded link, or the plain link marked "opens on the default". Then one row per choice, in the page's field order: the field as the page labels it, the chosen value, why (fact ID, inference from a fact ID, or neutral default), and whether that variant can be ordered. Give personalisation text exactly as it should be typed, with one alternative, only when the page has a personalisation field; otherwise write "Personalisation: none on this page". Never leave a choice for the buyer to decide when the page offers it; list a choice under buyer confirmations only when the page does not show it.
- Buyer confirmations: ownership, preferences, art permissions, proof, landed quote, delivery address, PO-box compatibility, destination, and deadline.

Compare each idea with generic swag in one sentence. State the specific personal fact that improves it; otherwise state that no demonstrated advantage exists.

State research limits, cost, and elapsed time separately from gift prices. Do not include rejected details.

## Examples

**Rich profile:** Repeated statements about annotating walking routes in a paper notebook can support a durable pocket notebook with an original trail-line motif. The item remains a concept until stock, destination delivery, and landed cost are verified.

**Pet:** A creator who often talks about walking their dog Luna can support a custom leash or bottle printed with Luna's name. It remains a concept until the item, delivery, and landed cost are verified.

**Preselected:** A creator who says bedtime is their favourite thing and never wears colour can get a silk pillowcase whose order card already reads: colour Natural White (inference from the colour fact), size Standard, monogram their initials, thread white for a tonal finish, so the buyer only confirms the address.

**Lasting favourite:** A creator who named a chocolate as a favourite three years ago, and never said otherwise, can get a hamper built around it, labelled "lasting favourite, last said 2022".

**Public figure:** A creator who keeps returning to a favourite novelist can support a special edition of that author's book. It remains a concept until the edition, delivery, and landed cost are verified.

**Thin profile:** One coffee-machine review does not establish a standing preference. Return `No good fit`.

**Changed profile:** Do not use an ended hobby or a premise contradicted by recent evidence.

**Multi-host:** Do not personalize one host’s gift with another host’s fact. Use shared facts only for a shared gift.

**Unavailable or late:** Find a feasible local equivalent with the same premise or return `No good fit`.

## Optional later evaluation design

If asked to evaluate gifting, compare personal gift plus ordinary outreach against identical outreach alone. Randomize comparable eligible creators and stratify by relationship, channel size, category, and country.

Keep sender, copy, offer, and send date comparable. Record opt-outs, address-request attrition, delivery failures, and delivery lag before analysis. Address consent and gift acceptance are not agreement to post or work with the brand.

Predefine a 14-day qualified-reply window and 30-day booking window. Analyze by original assignment. Report group sizes, uncertainty, undelivered gifts, and incremental cost per qualified reply and booking. Track voluntary posts separately; they are not deliverables.
