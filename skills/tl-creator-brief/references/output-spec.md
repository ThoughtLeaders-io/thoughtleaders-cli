# Output specification

## Who reads it

Default to a brand researching this creator. The brand needs telling who the
creator is; a creator needs no introduction to themselves. A version sent to the
creator is a downstream step, not this document.

## Stage 1, in chat

As soon as lanes B, C and D return, before anything is written up: best verbatim
quotes, audience-comment finds, resonance read. Raw material for a conversation
already in motion.

**Affordability belongs here and only here.** The competitor check already
returns the channel's recent deals, so an order-of-magnitude gap between what
the channel commands and what the brand spends is visible for free. Say it out
loud. Never write a rate, price or deal term into the document.

## Stage 2, the file

Markdown file, path returned. Rendered formats only on request.

Where the evidence supports more than one honest angle, present **two or three
directions** with the evidence behind each, then recommend one. A single
direction only when the evidence supports only one.

Sections, in this order. The connection is the spine, so it leads.

1. **Who is the creator.** A short profile in their own recorded words. Not
   subscriber counts, which the brand can already see.
2. **The personal connection.** Self-referential material with nothing to do
   with the brand yet: their business, how they work, the hobby, the dog. The
   section only we can write, because it comes out of a searchable transcript
   corpus.
3. **The brand connection.** That material bridged to the product. 5 to 8
   quote-bridges: they already believe X on the record, the product is X made
   practical. Title, date, timestamped link per quote. Any past organic mention
   of the brand or category leads this section.
4. **The audience.** What the comment sections say about the category or brand,
   and what resonance rewards and punishes. An audience asking for exactly this
   product leads.
5. **The use case.** One specific, believable way the creator or their team
   would use the product in their own work, built on the strongest format
   precedent. An exact on-camera workflow, not "you could use it".
6. **Directions.** The candidates with evidence, and a recommendation. At the
   research moment the fit verdict also goes at the very top in one line.
7. **A sample read** (pitch and booked only). Read skeleton and mandatories,
   opened from the use case, in the creator's diction. One per recommended
   direction. Labelled in the document as inspiration, never a script.
8. **Caveats.** Coverage, dropped quotes, competitor reads, affiliate
   uncertainty, comment-sample recency bias, anything unscoreable, anything
   inferred.

## Not this skill's job

Packaging: branded templates, discount codes, platform comments, email, sheets.
Those are one or two sentences in the caller's own prompt or routine. Keeping
them out is what keeps this skill reusable.
